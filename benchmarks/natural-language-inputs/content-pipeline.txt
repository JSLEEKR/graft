Create a 4-node content creation pipeline with memory:

Memory: PublishedArticles (max 3k tokens, file storage) with titles (list of strings), topics_covered (list of strings).

Input: Brief with topic (string), audience (string), tone (string), max_words (int). Max 800 tokens.

1. "Researcher" — claude-sonnet, 6k/3k budget
   - Reads: Brief, PublishedArticles
   - Produces ResearchNotes: key_points (list of strings), sources (list of strings), angle (string), avoid_topics (list of strings)

2. "Drafter" — claude-sonnet, 8k/4k budget
   - Reads: Brief, ResearchNotes
   - Produces Draft: title (string), body (string), word_count (int)

3. "Editor" — claude-opus, 10k/5k budget
   - Reads: Brief, Draft
   - Produces FinalArticle: title (string), body (string), word_count (int), edit_notes (list of strings)

4. "MetadataExtractor" — claude-haiku, 2k/1k budget
   - Reads: FinalArticle
   - Writes to: PublishedArticles
   - Produces ArticleMetadata: title (string), summary (string), tags (list of strings)

Flow: Researcher -> Drafter -> Editor -> MetadataExtractor -> done.

Edges:
- Researcher -> Drafter: select(key_points, angle), compact
- Drafter -> Editor: select(title, body)
- Editor -> MetadataExtractor: select(title), compact

Total budget: 40,000 tokens.

Generate: 4 agent files, 3 hooks, CLAUDE.md, settings.json, memory directory.
